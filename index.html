Section C — Reading comprehension (10 marks)

A Greener School

At the beginning of the school year, the Green Team at Seri Murni Secondary School noticed that many students threw recyclable items into ordinary rubbish bins. The team decided to start a campaign called “Small Actions, Big Difference”. Their first task was to place clearly labelled recycling bins near the canteen, library and science block.

Next, the students made short announcements during assembly. They explained that paper, clean plastic bottles and aluminium cans could be recycled. They also reminded everyone to rinse bottles before placing them in the bins. Some students were not interested at first because they thought recycling took too much time. The Green Team responded by organising a friendly competition between classes.

Every Friday, volunteers weighed each class’s recyclable materials and recorded the results on a noticeboard. The winning class received a certificate and could choose a tree to plant in the school garden. After two months, the amount of recyclable waste collected had increased. The school also used both sides of scrap paper for rough work. The team learnt that lasting change did not happen overnight, but small habits could make a real difference when many people worked together.

Your friend wants to know about your school’s English Week. Write an email to your friend. Include:
